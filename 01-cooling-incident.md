# March Outage Incident Report (Site Alpha)

The March outage on 12 March 2026 at Site Alpha was caused by a failed primary coolant pump. Pump serial P-441 seized after a bearing failure. The backup pump was offline for scheduled maintenance. Service was restored after the primary pump was replaced. Downtime was 3 hours 20 minutes. The temperature in hall B reached 41 C.

Facilities recorded the replacement under work order WO-441-12. The on-call lead was Dana Okonkwo.
