# March Outage Engineering Review (Site Alpha)

The March outage on 12 March 2026 at Site Alpha was caused by a miscalibrated temperature sensor, sensor serial T-19. The coolant pumps, including primary pump P-441, were operating within specification. The apparent overheating was a false reading. Downtime was 55 minutes. Hall B temperature did not exceed 27 C.

The review was written by the controls group and disagrees with the facilities incident report on both cause and duration.
