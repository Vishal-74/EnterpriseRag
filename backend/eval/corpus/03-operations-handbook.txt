Northwind Robotics operations handbook.

SEV-1 is a customer-facing total outage. SEV-2 is a major degradation. SEV-3 is a limited fault. SEV-4 is a request or cosmetic issue.
Escalate a SEV-1 to facilities within 15 minutes. The war room bridge is extension 4100.
Backups must be tested every Sunday at 02:00 UTC. Service account passwords rotate every 90 days.
The on-call handbook does not assign a cause to the March outage.
